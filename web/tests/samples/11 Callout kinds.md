# Callout kinds

> [!abstract] Abstract
> Summary text.

> [!todo]
> Things to do.

> [!success] Done
> It worked.

> [!question] A question?
> Maybe.

> [!failure]
> It failed.

> [!bug] A bug
> Steps to reproduce.

> [!example] Example
> An example body that is long enough to wrap onto a second row of the readable column inside the callout.

> [!quote] Quote
> A famous saying.

> [!faq]+ An open foldable callout
> Its body shows.

> [!note] Outer callout
> Outer body.
>
> > [!warning] Inner callout
> > Inner body.

> [!info] With a quote inside
> > A plain quote in a callout.

> [!tip] Title only with **bold** and `code`

> [!custom] Unknown type
> Falls back to a note.

The end.
